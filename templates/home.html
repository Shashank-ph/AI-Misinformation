AI-Generated Misinformation: Public Resources & Risk Assessment Tool

This site accompanies an MSc Cyber Security dissertation on AI-generated misinformation as a cybersecurity governance challenge. Two resources below are free and open to everyone. A third tool, the organisational Risk Matrix, requires a login because it is intended for cybersecurity and risk professionals.

Free Fact-Checker Resources

Independent, free fact-checking organisations for India, the European Union and the United States, plus an international tool for cross-checking claims that have already been investigated.

Recognising AI-Generated Content

Practical, content-specific checks for text, images, audio and video, plus a list of free or freemium AI-detection tools that can support — but never replace — human judgement.
