How to use these resources

  1. Search the claim, headline or a short quote directly on the fact-checker's site.
  2. For images or videos, use Google Fact Check Explorer or a reverse-image search first.
  3. If nothing has been published yet, treat the content as unverified rather than assuming it is true or false.
  4. Check more than one source where the claim is significant or could cause harm if wrong.
