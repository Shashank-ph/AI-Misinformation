This prototype supports the dissertation's Cybersecurity Governance Framework for AI-Generated Misinformation (Appendix N) by putting the verification and risk-calculation stages of that framework (Verify and Assess) into a simple, usable tool, alongside two resources aimed at strengthening public resilience.

1. Risk Matrix Tool

Score five likelihood indicators and three impact factors for a piece of suspected AI-generated content. The tool calculates the likelihood rating, impact rating and overall risk level using the 5×5 matrix from Appendix O, and shows the minimum required response.

Open Risk Matrix →

2. Free Fact-Checker Resources

A curated list of independent, free fact-checking organisations for India, the European Union and the United States, plus an international tool for cross-checking claims already investigated by verified publishers.

View Resources →

3. Recognising AI-Generated Content

Practical, content-specific checks for text, images, audio and video, plus a list of free or freemium AI-detection tools that can support — but never replace — human judgement.

View Guidance →
