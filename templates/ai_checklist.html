Recognising AI-Generated Content

Realistic AI-synthesised content can still increase belief in false claims even when it contains technical flaws. Visual or audio realism is therefore not evidence of authenticity on its own — always combine the checks below with source verification and, where a claim is significant, a check against the fact-checker resources.

Quick tips

  • Pause before sharing anything that triggers a strong, immediate emotional reaction — that reaction is often the intended effect of manipulated content.
  • Look for a plausible original source rather than relying on how many times something has been shared.
  • For anything involving money, credentials, safety or an urgent instruction from someone in authority, confirm it independently through a channel you already trust — never through the same channel the request arrived on.
